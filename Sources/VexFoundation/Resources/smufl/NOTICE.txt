SMuFL font metadata (subset)

These files contain the fontName, fontVersion, engravingDefaults and
glyphsWithAnchors fields of the SMuFL metadata.json files that ship with
the fonts:

- bravura-metadata.json: Bravura 1.392, Copyright (c) 2019, Steinberg Media
  Technologies GmbH. Source: @vexflow-fonts/bravura 1.0.2
  (https://github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura/tree/master/redist).
- petaluma-metadata.json: Petaluma 1.065, Copyright (c) 2018, Steinberg Media
  Technologies GmbH. Source: @vexflow-fonts/petaluma 1.0.1
  (https://github.com/steinbergmedia/petaluma/tree/master/redist).
- leland-metadata.json: Leland 0.77, Copyright (c) 2022, MuseScore BVBA.
  Source: @vexflow-fonts/leland 1.0.1 (https://github.com/MuseScoreFonts/Leland).

The fonts and their metadata are licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1. The license texts are in ../fonts/licenses
(bravura-LICENSE.txt, petaluma-LICENSE.txt, leland-LICENSE.txt).

Regenerate with tools/extract_smufl_metadata.py.
